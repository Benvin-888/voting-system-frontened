We stand for

A modern, secure, and transparent voting system built for every citizen.

The Kirinyaga Electronic Voting System is a secure digital platform designed to facilitate transparent, fair, and efficient elections within the county. The system ensures that every registered voter can participate safely while maintaining vote confidentiality and integrity.

Frequently asked questions
Is my vote private?
Yes. Votes are encrypted and stored without any personally identifiable information. No one can link a vote back to you.
Can I vote more than once?
No. The system checks your unique voter ID and one‑time voting number – duplicates are automatically rejected.
What if I lose my voting number?
Contact our support team immediately. They will verify your identity and issue a replacement securely.
When does voting open and close?
Voting starts at 6:00 AM and ends at 5:00 PM on election day. Exact dates are announced by the electoral commission.
Can observers see who I voted for?
No. Observers only see aggregate totals and process logs – never individual votes.
